# Integral Geometry Science (IGS)

> Kobe University deep-tech spinout (founded 2012-04-02) commercializing Dr. Kenjiro Kimura's
> analytical solution to the inverse problem of wave scattering. IGS builds measurement hardware
> and imaging software that reveals the interior of an object without cutting it open: microwave
> mammography, non-destructive battery current-density imaging, walk-through security screening,
> and rebar corrosion/fracture inspection. Headquartered in Kobe, Hyogo, Japan.

## Solutions (products, not APIs)
- [Microwave Mammography](https://www.igs-group.com/en/solutions/mammography): painless, radiation-free breast imaging effective in dense breast tissue; Sakigake-designated by Japan's MHLW
- [Battery Inspection](https://www.igs-group.com/en/solutions/battery): non-destructive imaging of current-density distribution inside rechargeable cells
- [Walk-through Security](https://www.igs-group.com/en/solutions/security): concealed hazardous-material detection
